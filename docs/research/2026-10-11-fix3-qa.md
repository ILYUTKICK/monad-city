## Source audit disposition

The provisional audit identified chooser/legend overlap, an unbounded Ecosystem layer, incorrect visible counts and stale hover. All four were corrected. Performance is now measured with the remaining budget gap. The explicit hidden-parent raycast guard remains necessary and present.

## Remaining limits and reference differences

- Draw-call budget remains unmet. No physical mobile or weak-GPU benchmark was run.
- Reflections are static outline-aligned contact silhouettes and short ripples, rather than detailed reflected buildings. Water looks flatter than the painted concept; no SSR/bloom was added.
- Procedural building volumes, courtyards, rectangular islands and street markings have less architectural detail than the concept. This is not pixel-identical reproduction.
- Explicit Show all can produce dense routes and cull colliding labels. Default focus stays bounded; exact records remain in Passport.
- Reduced motion was checked in source (direct camera/dimming settlement, skipped intro). Browser controls cannot emulate the OS preference; runtime preference verification remains pending. Full accessibility and exhaustive selection of all 176 projects were not performed.
- Graph regression is functional, not pixel-diffed. Qwen was checked on the existing Beefy path; no AI/backend integration changed.
